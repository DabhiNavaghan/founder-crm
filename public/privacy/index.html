Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

FoundReach (also shown in the product as FounderFlow) is an outreach CRM available at foundreach.navaghandabhi.dev. This page explains what information the service collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up or sign in, we receive your name, email address and, if you use Google sign-in, your basic Google profile (name, email address and profile picture). We do not receive your Google password and we do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar or files.
  • Workspace content. The companies, contacts, notes, outreach messages, activity records and outreach profile you add to your workspace, including spreadsheets you choose to import.
  • Technical data. Standard request data such as IP address and browser type, processed by our hosting and authentication providers to deliver and secure the service.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store your workspace and show it only to you.
  • To generate outreach drafts when you ask for one. The contact, company and context you select for that draft are sent to our AI provider for that purpose.
  • To operate, maintain and troubleshoot the service.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

We rely on the following providers, which process data on our behalf:

  • Clerk — authentication and account management, including Google sign-in.
  • Supabase — database storage for your workspace content.
  • OpenAI — generation of message drafts you request.
  • Vercel — application hosting.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, FoundReach uses your Google name, email address and profile picture only to create and identify your account. This data is not shared with third parties other than the providers listed above as needed to run the service, and it is not used for any other purpose.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account information and workspace content for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by emailing the address below, and we will do so within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and workspace records are restricted so that each account can read and change only its own data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
